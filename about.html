ORGANISATIONAL BACKGROUND

EDNA FOODS Ltd.

EDNA FOODS Ltd. is a catering and food services company serving customers in Kampala and beyond. The organisation focuses on helping individuals, families and organisations plan occasions through catering, food presentation and event support.

Its service context includes weddings, introduction ceremonies, corporate functions, birthdays, family gatherings and other celebrations. Each event has its own requirements, including the date, venue, expected number of guests, preferred menu and service arrangements.

The EDNA FOODS brand is presented through its burgundy, brown and gold identity, supported by clean white and light-grey layouts. The brand message is centred on making every occasion memorable through thoughtful catering and service.

EXAMINATION CASE STUDY

Digitising the event request and quotation process

The project examines how a catering and food services organisation can coordinate customer requests and booking information through an integrated digital system.

Case study context

An event enquiry can involve several details: customer contacts, event type, date, venue, guest numbers, food preferences, special instructions and the services requested. Management then needs to review the information and prepare a quotation that reflects the customer's requirements.

Problem being addressed

When requests, correspondence, cost details and booking updates are handled in separate places, it can be harder to retrieve a complete record, follow the status of an enquiry and prepare consistent reports. This project uses that operational challenge as the problem context for the examination system.

Proposed digital solution

The proposed system connects a public information website to an authenticated application. Customers can register and submit event requests. EDNA FOODS administrators can review requests, update statuses, prepare quotations, manage bookings and generate reports using records stored in a MySQL database.

Expected value

The system is intended to centralise customer and request information, make request status easier to track, support structured quotation preparation and provide a searchable record for management reporting.
