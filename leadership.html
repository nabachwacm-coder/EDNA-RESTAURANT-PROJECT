PEOPLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Leadership and the people behind the service

Good event delivery depends on planning, coordination, food preparation and attentive service. This page introduces the key responsibilities that support an event.

TEAM STRUCTURE

Working together to deliver an event

The role descriptions below are an illustrative structure for the EDNA FOODS website and examination case study.

01

Company management

Oversees customer enquiries, service decisions, quotations, bookings and general coordination of the business.

02

Event coordination

Coordinates event requirements, schedules, venue information and communication between the customer and the service team.

03

Kitchen and catering team

Plans food preparation and supports the delivery of menu items according to the approved event requirements.

04

Service and logistics team

Supports setup, delivery, serving arrangements and other assigned operational tasks for an event.

Leadership profile note: Individual names, portraits, job titles and biographies have not been provided. This page therefore describes functional roles rather than inventing staff identities. Official profiles can be added when management confirms the details and approves publication.

OUR APPROACH

Clear responsibilities. Coordinated service.

For the digital system being developed, management will be able to review customer requests, prepare quotations and coordinate bookings. Future system phases can extend this workflow to supplier communication and employee task assignments.
